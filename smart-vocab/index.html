Privacy Policy for Lexio

Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short: Lexio has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The words you add, your progress, your photos and your voice notes stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us, because we do not run a server. The only time the app reaches the internet is when you tap the dictionary button, and when Google Play checks whether a newer version of the app exists.

1. Who we are

Lexio ("the Application") is a vocabulary learning app for Android, published on Google Play under the package name com.akhror.smart_vocab_v3. It was previously called Smart Vocabulary. The Application is developed by Akhror Juraev ("we", "us", "our").

By installing and using the Application you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. We do not collect your personal data

The Application does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide a name, an email address or a phone number. It does not collect your device identifiers, your advertising ID, your location, your contacts or your installed apps.

We do not operate a server that receives your content. We therefore have no copy of anything you create in the Application and no way to identify you.

Specifically, the Application contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking software of any kind.

3. What the Application stores on your device

Everything below is written to the Application's own private storage on your phone. It is not readable by other apps, it is never uploaded, and Android deletes all of it when you uninstall the Application.

What Where
Words you add, with their meanings, examples, mnemonics, synonyms, notes and the groups and languages you file them under A local database on your device
Your learning progress: correct and wrong answers, answer times, review schedule and per-skill statistics The same local database
Photos and voice notes you attach to a word Files in the Application's private documents folder
Your settings: interface language, active learning language, pronunciation and reminder preferences Android's app preferences storage

4. Device permissions and why they are needed

Microphone

The microphone is used in two places, and only while you are actively using them:

  • Pronunciation practice. When you take a speaking exercise, your speech is passed to your device's own Android speech recognition service so that it can be turned into text and compared with the expected word. That service is part of your device, supplied by your device manufacturer or by Google, and its handling of the audio is governed by their privacy policy, not ours. The Application does not save this audio.
  • Voice notes. If you record your own voice note for a word, the recording is saved as a file in the Application's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded. You can delete it at any time from the word's screen.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a word, you choose it through Android's own photo picker or take it with your camera. A copy is stored in the Application's private storage. The Application has no access to the rest of your photo library and does not upload anything.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily study reminder, the Application asks Android to show a notification at the time you chose. The reminder is scheduled entirely on your device; no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Internet

See the next section. The Application works fully offline apart from the two cases described there.

5. The only two times data leaves your device

a) Dictionary lookup — optional, and only when you ask for it

When you are adding or editing an English word, you can tap the dictionary button to fill in a definition automatically. Only then does the Application send that single word to the free public dictionary service at dictionaryapi.dev and show you what comes back.

No account details, device identifiers or other content are sent with the request, because the Application holds none. We do not store the request or the result on any server of ours; the result is simply placed in the form for you to edit or discard. The dictionary service is operated by a third party and processes the request under its own terms. If you never tap the button, no word is ever sent.

b) Checking for app updates

The Application asks Google Play whether a newer version of it has been published, so it can offer you the update. This check is performed by Google Play services on your device and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No content from the Application is included in it.

6. We do not share or sell anything

Because we do not receive your data in the first place, there is nothing for us to sell, rent, trade or share. We have no advertising partners and no analytics partners. The only third parties involved are the ones named in section 5, and only for the narrow purposes described there.

7. Keeping your information safe

Your content is protected by Android itself: the Application writes to its own private storage area, which other applications on your device cannot read. Nothing is transmitted to us, so there is no account of yours to be exposed in a breach on our side.

The security of your device is in your hands: anyone who can unlock your phone can open the Application and see your words, photos and voice notes.

8. Deleting your data

You are always in control:

  • Delete a single word, group or language inside the Application, and its photos and voice notes go with it.
  • Clear the Application's storage from Android Settings to erase everything while keeping it installed.
  • Uninstall the Application, and Android removes all of its data from your device.

Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from us and no deletion request to submit.

9. Children's privacy

The Application is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will act on it.
